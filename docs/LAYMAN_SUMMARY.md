## 1. The Simple Story: How to Explain This Project in 60 Seconds

Imagine a massive office skyscraper where the internet suddenly dies for half the building. 

To fix it, you hire an AI detective (an LLM like Gemini).

* **The Baseline Detective (Without a Map):**  
  You give the detective a set of tools (ping, checking wires, reading command screens), but **no floor plan**.  
  The detective has to knock on random doors one by one: *"Is your wire broken? No? Okay, let me walk to the 5th floor... how about your wire? No?"*  
  Eventually, after 14 different checks and over a minute of wandering, the detective finds the loose cable in Room 302 and fixes it.

* **Our New Detective (With the Topology Blueprint):**  
  We give the detective the exact same tools, but we also hand them **a blueprint of the entire building** showing every router, switch, and cable connection.  
  Before knocking on any door, the detective looks at the map: *"Wait, traffic flows from Leaf Switch 1 through Spine 1 to Host 3. The break has to be on this specific path."*  
  The detective walks straight to the relevant connection and solves the exact same problem in **42 seconds** instead of 64 seconds, with fewer questions and less wandering.

**The Core Finding:** Giving AI an architectural map makes it **34% faster**, cuts its thinking token costs by **10%**, and prevents it from getting lost in big networks.
